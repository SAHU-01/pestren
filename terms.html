2What Pestren is

Pestren is land due-diligence infrastructure. It assembles public and licensed datasets about a parcel — soil surveys, climate and hydrology records, cadastral and ownership data, land cover, hazard and environmental records, satellite imagery — and produces cited reports and machine-assisted analysis.

4Accuracy, sources and AI output

Our reports rest on third-party public data — federal, state, county and municipal records, and their foreign equivalents. That data can be outdated, incomplete, mis-georeferenced or simply wrong, and we do not control it. We show you the source and vintage of each fact so you can judge it. We do not warrant the accuracy, completeness or currency of any dataset or of any report.

The Assistant uses a large language model. It is instructed to answer only from cited facts and to say when something is missing, but like all such systems it can be wrong, can misread a figure, and can present a regional average as if it described your parcel. Treat its output as a draft to be checked against the citations, never as a finding. Where a report distinguishes a parcel-level measurement from a regional aggregate, that distinction is material and you must respect it.
