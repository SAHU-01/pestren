Refund & Cancellation Policy

What you are charged, when, and exactly when we give it back. Every plan opens with a two-day free trial, so you can read this before a single rupee or cent moves.

Effective 23 September 2026 · Version 1.0 · Forms part of the Terms & Conditions

1Where things stand today

Paid plans are live

Pestren sells subscriptions. Every plan opens with a 2-day free trial. A payment method is collected when the trial starts, nothing is charged during it, and cancelling before it ends costs you nothing at all. The first charge falls on day 3.

Browsing the demo — the seeded fields and the sample reports — stays free and collects no payment method. You only reach a payment screen by choosing a plan or unlocking a report.

Payments are taken by Paddle.com, our authorised reseller and Merchant of Record. Your contract of sale is with Paddle, who also handle tax and invoicing; Paddle’s buyer terms apply alongside this policy. Charges appear on your statement under Paddle’s descriptor rather than ours — that is not an unknown charge.

3Reports — one-off purchases

A $5 unlock is a digital product delivered immediately on payment: it opens one parcel’s full report on screen, as a PDF, and to the Assistant, permanently. Once delivered the purchase is final, because the cost of producing it — the cadastral lookups, imagery processing and model work — is incurred at that moment and cannot be recovered.

We will refund you in full, without argument, if:

  • the report failed to generate, or was never delivered;
  • you were charged more than once for the same parcel;
  • the report is materially incomplete — a section we advertised for your region returned no data because of a fault on our side;
  • you were charged for coverage we do not in fact have in your region;
  • the charge was not authorised by you.

We will not refund a complete report because its conclusions were unwelcome, because a public dataset your county maintains is thin or outdated, or because you changed your mind after reading it. Coverage depth by region is stated before you buy, and every fact is cited so you can see its source and vintage.

If a report is partly unavailable — some sections delivered, others blocked by an upstream source — tell us and we will either regenerate it at no charge once the source recovers, or refund the proportion that failed. You choose.

4Plan subscriptions

4.1The free trial

Every plan starts with a 2-day free trial. You are not charged during it. Cancel before it ends and you pay nothing — there is no trial fee and no partial charge. If you do nothing, the first payment is taken on day 3 at the price shown when you signed up.

4.2Cancelling

Cancel any time, in one step. No email required, no retention call, no cancellation fee. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the period you have already paid for — you keep full access until then, and you are not billed again.

4.3How much comes back, and why

You have 7 days from your first charge

A refund can be claimed within 7 days of your first charge. After that, a period you have entered is yours and is not refunded.

One carve-out, on annual plans only. Whole months you paid for and never started are not a refund for dissatisfaction — they are money taken for service not yet delivered. Those come back whenever you cancel, inside the window or long outside it. We are not going to keep eleven months of an annual fee from someone who stopped using Pestren in month one.

Inside the window, the rule is one line:

refund = what you paid × (1 − the share of it you used)

Your plan comes with a monthly allowance of four things. We take whichever one you have consumed the most of, as a fraction of its allowance, and refund the rest.

The highest measure governs because it reflects what you actually took. Someone who touched one hectare but spent every report has had the expensive part of the product, and a low hectare figure should not rescue that.

Worked example. Starter, $10 for the month. You analysed 2 of your 4 hectares and asked 100 of your 200 questions. The highest measure is 50%, so you keep half the month and $5.00 comes back.

Usage is counted cumulatively across the month — distinct land analysed, not what happens to be in your workspace at the end. Deleting a field does not un-spend its report.

Why a share and not a list of charges

An earlier draft of this policy deducted our measured costs — so much per hectare, so much per question. We abandoned it, and the reason is worth stating plainly: those costs are tiny. A grounded assistant answer costs us fractions of a cent. Storing a year of satellite rasters for a 500-hectare account costs less than a penny.

Deducting that from a refund would be theatre, and any larger number would be invented. So this is an honest statement about value delivered, not a reconstruction of our costs: you used a third of what you bought, you keep a third, you get two thirds back. Every plan behaves identically.

Work out your own figure with the refund calculator — it runs this exact rule and shows which measure governed. If the calculator and this policy ever disagree, this policy wins and we fix the calculator. And if the number still looks wrong to you, §6 is how you reach a human who can override it.

4.4Renewals

  • Paddle emails a reminder before each renewal, stating the amount and the date. For annual plans this is at least 7 days ahead; for monthly plans, 3 days.
  • Renewals are assessed by the same rule as any other period — see §4.3. On a monthly plan, usage worth more than the month returns nothing; on an annual plan the months you never started always come back.
  • If we raise the price, existing subscribers get 30 days’ notice and the change applies only from the next renewal. Cancel before it and you never pay the new price.
  • Changing seats or plan mid-period is pro-rated by Paddle; an increase is charged immediately, a decrease takes effect at the next renewal.

4.5When we will refund a renewal anyway

  • Sustained outage — the service is unavailable for more than 72 consecutive hours in a billing period: a pro-rata credit or refund for the affected days.
  • You cancelled and we billed you anyway: full refund.
  • A material change to the Terms that you reject under Terms §15: pro-rata refund of the unused term.
  • Duplicate or unauthorised charge: full refund.

5Enterprise seats

Enterprise is a self-serve plan sold per seat at the price in §2, with a four-seat minimum, on the same trial, cancellation and refund terms as every other plan. A seat is a login; the 100-field and 500-hectare limits belong to the subscription and are shared across every seat on it.

Separate volume, API or custom-coverage arrangements remain available by signed order form. Where one exists it prevails over this policy. Absent a contrary term: invoices are payable within 30 days, either party may decline renewal with 30 days’ notice before the term ends, and fees for a term already begun are non-refundable except for unused prepaid volume, which we refund pro-rata if we terminate for convenience.

6How to ask for a refund

Email core@pestren.com with the charge date, the amount, and what went wrong. That is the whole process.

  • We acknowledge within 2 business days and decide within 5 business days.
  • Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5–10 business days; your bank may take longer to show it.
  • If we refuse, we tell you why, in writing, and point to the clause we relied on.

Refunds are made in the currency charged. We do not reimburse exchange-rate movement or your bank’s foreign-transaction fee, as neither reaches us.

Not happy with the number? Ask a human.

The bands in §4.3 are a formula, and a formula cannot know that your month went sideways. If the figure looks wrong to you — or you are simply not sure it is right — say so. Reply to the refund decision, email core@pestren.com, or ask us to schedule a refund call and we will talk it through.

Someone will then look at your account independently of the calculator and decide on the facts, and we can settle on an amount outside the bands where that is the fair answer. Asking costs you nothing and never reduces what the formula already offered.

7Failed payments and chargebacks

If a payment fails we retry and email you. If it is still unpaid after 7 days we suspend the subscription; your data is untouched and access resumes the moment payment succeeds. After 30 days unpaid we may close the subscription, and the retention periods in the Privacy Policy then apply.

If something looks wrong, please contact us before raising a chargeback — we will almost always just refund it. A chargeback raised without contacting us first may result in suspension while the dispute is open.

8Your data when you cancel

Cancelling a subscription does not delete anything. Your fields, parcels, reports and history stay in your account and stay readable — you lose the paid allowances, not your work. You cannot add fields beyond the free limits until you subscribe again, but nothing already there is removed. Reports you unlocked with a $5 purchase remain yours to read and download permanently, subscription or not. If you delete your account instead, the Privacy Policy §7 retention periods apply, and you may export everything first.

9Your statutory rights

Nothing in this policy limits rights you have under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019, the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020, or mandatory consumer law where you live — including, for EU and UK consumers, the 14-day right of withdrawal, which you expressly ask us to override for a specific report by requesting immediate delivery, and which you acknowledge is lost once that report is delivered.
