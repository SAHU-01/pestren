Privacy Policy

Written from an audit of what this codebase actually collects — every claim below is traceable to a table, a route or a line of source, not to a template.

Effective 23 September 2026 · Version 1.0 · Governed by the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (India)

1Who we are

Pestren is operated by Ankita Sahu, a sole proprietor based in India (“Pestren”, “we”, “us”). We are the Data Fiduciary for the personal data described here under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (“DPDP Act”), the controller for visitors in the EU and UK, and the equivalent under California law.

Contact for any privacy question, request or complaint — including our Grievance Officer under §13 of the DPDP Act: core@pestren.com. Postal address: F-21, B-6, Phase-1, Metrocity Apartment, Nayapalli, Bhubaneswar, Odisha 751012, India.

This policy covers pestren.com, interface.pestren.com, socials.pestren.com and our API. When Pestren incorporates, the successor entity assumes these obligations unchanged and we will update this section.

2The short version

We collect the minimum the product needs. We do not sell personal data, we do not run advertising or cross-site tracking, we set no analytics or advertising cookies, and we do not train any model on your data.

The land data in Pestren is land you nominate — boundaries you draw, import or select. We never collect your device’s location in the background. The one exception is deliberate and opt-in: if you press the “use my location” button, your browser asks your permission and we use the coordinates it returns to centre the map and check whether we cover that spot. See §3.8.

3What we collect, and why

3.1If you join the waitlist or send feedback

  • Your country (required) — to understand regional interest and to determine whether we may lawfully offer the service in your region.
  • Your feedback and the sentiment you pick (required) — product research.
  • Your email (optional) — stored only if you tick the newsletter box. If you do not tick it, no email is stored at all.
  • A salted, irreversible hash of your IP address — for spam and abuse limiting. We do not store the IP itself.

3.2If you create an account

  • Your email address, and first/last name if you choose to give them. Sign-in is handled by Clerk; if you use Google sign-in, Google confirms your identity to Clerk and we receive your email and name, nothing more.
  • We never receive or store your password. Clerk holds and hashes credentials; they do not reach our servers.

3.3The land data you create

  • Boundaries of parcels and fields you draw, import (.geojson, .kml, .gpx, .zip, .gpkg) or select from a public cadastre.
  • Attributes you add — name, crop, colour, tags, and free-text notes.
  • Management events you log — planting, fertiliser, irrigation, pest and disease observations, harvest and yield.
  • Files you upload — photographs for pest diagnosis, documents in the interior module.
  • A version history of every change, retained as an audit trail. See §7 for what this means when you ask for deletion.

3.4When you use the Assistant

Each question is answered by a third-party reasoning model, currently DeepSeek, whose servers are in the People’s Republic of China. Two things are sent, and only these two:

  • The question you typed. This is your own text — if you type an address, a name or a private note into the question box, it is transmitted.
  • The land report for the parcel you selected, assembled from public datasets (soil surveys, climate records, cadastral and land-cover data, hazard maps).

Your email address, account identifier, IP address and the contents of your account are not sent. We do not train models on your data, and we contractually prohibit our providers from doing so. Your questions are kept in your own browser’s storage, not on our servers, unless you publish a chat (§3.6).

3.5If you are an invited tester

We store your invite code, the IP address it was first redeemed from, and an append-only log of each action taken on it — the action type, the IP, a short excerpt of the action (up to 300 characters, which may include the question you asked) and the time. The IP binding exists so a single code cannot be forwarded to a group and used by thirty people; it is a quota control, not an identity check.

3.6If you publish a chat

Please read this one

Publishing a chat creates a permanent, public, read-only page that anyone with the link can open. There is no sign-in on it. The published transcript contains everything in that conversation — your questions, the answers, the evidence and the coordinates.

Do not publish a chat containing anything you would not put on a public website — and note that a land report can name a parcel’s owner, which is someone else’s personal data.

To take one down, use the Unpublish button beside the link. The key that authorises removal is stored in the browser you published from, so if you have cleared that browser’s data, email us the link instead and we will delete it.

3.7Technical data

  • Server logs and rate-limit counters — IP address, timestamp, endpoint, status. Used to keep the service up and to stop abuse.
  • Aggregate page views via Vercel Web Analytics — cookieless, with no cross-site identifier and no profile of you.

3.8If you use the location button

Pestren has two opt-in ways to find where you are: the crosshair button in the search bar, and the geolocate control on the map. Neither runs on its own — each is a button you press, and your browser asks your permission before anything happens. Decline, or never press them, and no location is ever read.

  • The search-bar button reads your position once, at high accuracy. We send those coordinates to our own API to ask one question: do we have land data covering this point?
  • The map control keeps a position marker following you while it is switched on, so you can see yourself against a field boundary. Those readings stay in your browser and are not sent to us.

We do not store your device location against your account, log it, build a location history, use it for advertising or profiling, or share it with anyone. The coverage lookup is answered and discarded; what persists is only the field you then choose to create. Turn it off at any time in your browser’s site settings.

3.9What we never collect

Your location in the background (only the opt-in button in §3.8 ever reads it, and we do not keep the result) · your phone number · your postal address (unless you email it to us) · your payment card details · any special-category data — health, financial, biometric, religion, caste or political opinion. If we do not need it, we do not ask for it.

On card details specifically: your card is entered into Paddle’s checkout, never into ours, and Paddle is the Merchant of Record for the sale. Card numbers never touch our servers and we cannot see them. What we receive and store from Paddle is only what is needed to know what you are entitled to: a Paddle customer and subscription ID, the plan, its status, the seat count, and the renewal and trial dates. Not the card, not the last four digits, not the expiry.

4Our legal basis

  • Consent — the waitlist, the newsletter, and any optional upload. You may withdraw it at any time, and withdrawing is as easy as giving it.
  • Performance of a contract — running your account and delivering the reports you request.
  • Legitimate uses / legitimate interests — security, abuse prevention, rate limiting, and keeping our own financial records.

For EU and UK visitors these map to Articles 6(1)(a), (b) and (f) GDPR respectively.

What a basemap provider can infer

Drawing a map means asking someone for map tiles, and the request necessarily says which part of the world you are looking at. The tile providers above — MapTiler, OpenStreetMap, USGS and NLSC — therefore see the area you are viewing and your IP address, as any website showing a map does. They do not receive your account, your field boundaries, or anything you have drawn. We have no way to show you a map without this, and we would rather state it than let a table imply otherwise.

We will also disclose data where the law requires it, and to a successor entity if Pestren is incorporated, merged or acquired — in which case this policy travels with the data.

6International transfers

We operate from India and our infrastructure is largely in the United States, so your data is transferred abroad. Where the transfer is out of the EU or UK we rely on the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses. The DPDP Act permits transfers to any country the Central Government has not restricted; if India restricts a destination we use, we will move or stop that processing.

Called out plainly

Using the Assistant transfers your question and the public parcel report to DeepSeek in the People’s Republic of China. If you would rather this did not happen, do not use the Assistant — every other part of Pestren works without it.

An honest note about the audit trail

Field history is designed to be immutable — it deliberately outlives the field it describes, so a boundary’s lineage can be replayed. Deleting a single field therefore removes it from your workspace but keeps its history. Deleting your account erases both.

If you need a specific history record purged while keeping your account, email us and we will do it.

8Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you have the right to:

  • Access a summary of the personal data we process about you and who we have shared it with.
  • Correct, complete, update or erase your personal data.
  • Grievance redressal — raise a complaint with us first; we will respond within 30 days. If unsatisfied you may escalate to the Data Protection Board of India.
  • Nominate another person to exercise these rights if you die or become incapacitated.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, without giving a reason.

We extend the same handling to everyone regardless of where they live. If you are in the EU or UK you additionally have rights to portability, restriction and objection, and may complain to your supervisory authority. If you are in the United States, your state may give you rights to know, access, correct, delete, obtain a portable copy, and opt out of the sale or sharing of personal data and of targeted advertising and profiling — California (CCPA/CPRA), Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah, Texas, Oregon, Montana and a growing list of others. We do not sell or share personal data, run targeted advertising, or profile you, so there is nothing to opt out of; the remaining rights we honour for every US resident regardless of state, and we do not require you to prove which state you are in. We will never charge you or degrade your service for exercising any of this.

Exercise any right at core@pestren.com.

8.1How to delete your data

A right to erasure is worth nothing if the steps are hidden, so here they are in full. Nothing below requires you to justify the request.

  • One field — delete it in the Fields panel. It leaves your workspace immediately. Its edit history is retained; see the note in §7.
  • Everything, including the account — email core@pestren.com with the subject “Delete my account” from the address you signed up with. We confirm within 2 business days and erase within 30 days: your sign-in record, fields, parcels, reports, edit history, chats and invite usage. No retention call, no exit survey.
  • One specific history record, keeping your account — email core@pestren.com naming the field and the change. We purge it.
  • Waitlist or newsletter only — reply “unsubscribe” to any email, or write to core@pestren.com.

Two things survive an account deletion, and we would rather say so than surprise you. Financial records — invoices and the fact a payment happened — are kept for 8 years because Indian tax law requires it; they live with Paddle as Merchant of Record and with us in ledger form, and contain no card details. Backups roll off on their own cycle within 30 days, and we do not restore deleted data from them.

To delete the account you hold with Paddle for billing purposes, contact Paddle directly through their privacy policy. We can erase our copy of your billing identifiers, but Paddle is a separate controller for the payment itself.

9Security

TLS on every connection; passwords held and hashed by Clerk, never by us; land data scoped to its owner and checked on every read and write; rate limiting and per-account quotas; secrets in environment variables, never in the browser; IP addresses on the marketing site stored only as salted hashes.

No system is perfectly secure, and we do not claim otherwise. If a breach occurs that is likely to affect you, we will notify you and the Data Protection Board of India without undue delay.

10Cookies

Pestren sets only the cookies it needs to work — to keep you signed in and to check your invite. There are no advertising, analytics or tracking cookies anywhere in the product. Every cookie is itemised in the Cookie Policy.

11Children

Pestren is a professional tool and is not directed at anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children, and we do not carry out behavioural advertising or tracking of any user, which the DPDP Act prohibits for children.

12Changes

We will update this policy when the product changes, and revise the effective date above. For a change that materially reduces your protections — a new category of data, a new purpose, a new country — we will notify account holders by email before it takes effect.

Previous versions of this policy are kept and available on request at core@pestren.com. A policy you agreed to is part of the deal you made, so you should be able to read the version that applied at the time.
