01 · The industry problem

The world spends $10 billion a year valuing land, and still misprices it.

Evaluating one parcel means answering 80-plus interdependent questions — soil, water, crop yield, legal and UCC status, insurance, the owner’s credit. Today that is 30 to 60 days and up to $6,000 a report, stitched together by hand. The data exists. Nothing connects it.

  • $9.94B spent a year valuing property Real estate valuation services market, 2025
  • $116.4B of US property already distressed MSCI balance of distress, Q1 2025 · up 23% year on year
  • $10–20K diligence on one complex parcel Buyer pays up front, and forfeits it if the deal dies

Nine generations of my family have owned farmland. Answering one question about my own field — could it support a new crop — still cost $10,000 and two months, across soil, legal and financial records that never connected. If it is that hard on land I know, it is worse for a lender evaluating land blind.

Sources — MSCI Capital Trends, US Distress Tracker · HTF Market Intelligence · Research and Markets

06 · FAQ

        
Questions, answered.

        
The things partners and growers ask us most — starting with where the intelligence actually comes from.

      


      

        

          
          

            Pestren runs on an open-source foundation model, trained and grounded on openly available, authoritative datasets. Global layers come from ESA Sentinel (satellite), NASA (weather), and ISRIC SoilGrids (soil), plus specialist references like the Insecticide Resistance Action Committee (IRAC) for resistance management. On top of that we use each region's official land-parcel and crop datasets:
            
    
              
  • Europe — LPIS (Land Parcel Identification System), CAP declarations
    • 
              
  • USA — USDA Cropland Data Layer (CDL)
    • 
              
  • Brazil — CONAB, MapBiomas
    • 
              
  • India — state agriculture departments, ICAR institutes, FASAL outputs, plus PMFBY and the Digital Crop Survey where access permits (both restricted)
    • 
              
  • Australia — ABARES
    • 
              
  • Canada — AAFC Crop Inventory
    • 
            

            On top of that public base we layer field data and verification points contributed by our partner FPOs (Farmer Producer Organizations), so answers are checked against the real ground — not just the literature. Every answer names the sources it used.
          

        

        

          
          

            Weather updates daily, satellite refreshes on its revisit cycle, and risk is scored weekly. Every value carries its own timestamp so you always know how recent it is.
          

        

        

          
          

            Every fact is tagged with a confidence tier — literature-only, field-verified, or lab-confirmed — so you always see whether it's backed by published sources, ground truth from the field, or lab results.
          

        

        

          
          

            No. Your questions and coordinates are never used to train models. Field data contributed through FPO partnerships is used only with consent, to improve verification coverage.
          

        

        

          
          

            Our vision is simple: any crop, any geography. At launch, Pestren supports farms growing food crops across the United States and India. We're already expanding our coverage and plan to add more countries and continents beginning next quarter.

          

        

        

          
          

            A free tier plus a metered API, with volume plans for enterprise. Grower access is kept low-friction on purpose.
